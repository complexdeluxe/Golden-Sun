## Details that change the port

- **Number King.** `its me` at the first mode prompt reveals the target and guess count and permits an integer target override, including after each hard shift. Declining keeps the generated target. `complex` selects hard (1–100000), otherwise easy (1–100). Hard shifts before guesses 20, 40, 60 and 80. Keep the original hint bands and grade thresholds. Guess 100 can win with no printed grade, and some messages display one excess guess. Proposed fix for the port is to report the actual accepted count and extend the last initial-loop grade through guess 100 (easy D, hard B). The masochist key is a coming-soon message.
- **Dark Moon.** Stage 1 starts at 0 seconds. Stages 2–5 begin at 40, 80, 120 and 160 seconds, and 6–10 at 200, 250, 300, 350 and 400 seconds. Surviving to roughly 7:20 starts the victory fade. The source ultimately calls the death routine. Complete successfully at the end of that sequence, with a visible assisted marker if invincibility was used. Complex should compare the final-stage pace and fade. Pre-run Codes accepts `The Author of the Continental Page`. Preserve the first-five-seconds legacy chord (vertical bar and tilde) where practical and the fatal result for a wrong submitted phrase on that route. Cancel dismisses safely. Repository MP3s have no supplied Roblox asset IDs. Leave playback disabled pending authorized IDs.
- **Take Flight!** Durability, Power, Mobility and Precision start at 2. Ordinary spending raises a stat to 5 and refunding lowers it to 1. Score must strictly exceed 200, 400, 750 and 1000 to advance the requirement, then each active tick above 2000 awards a point intentionally. `complexscore` sets an integer score. `complexdura` sets durability past the ordinary cap. Source tables include tier 6 (9999 HP, 999 regen, zero reduction). Proposed fallback for 7+ retains the entered tier and uses tier 6 derived values. Reject invalid/out-of-domain values visibly. Preserve the two original chords where practical and expose both codes in Codes. The source clears the bullet group when an offscreen bullet expires. Have Complex review any change to firing cadence or projectile cleanup.
- **Estimate.** Number King needs one text-driven game screen and has the fewest moving pieces. Dark Moon adds one fixed-step simulation plus a real-time final-stage run. Take Flight! adds a second simulation, four stat tables to apply, projectiles and multiple hazard behaviors. Integrate Number King first, then exercise repeated switches with Dark Moon, then add Take Flight! and repeat the full regression pass. The first end-to-end playtest will inform a calendar estimate.
